# **FinSight Financial Distribution Analytics**
A Power BI financial analytics project exploring revenue, profitability, expenses, and operational performance for FinSight Distribution & Consumer Products Ltd.

# **Project Overview**
This project turns financial transaction data into interactive Power BI dashboards and a structured Profit & Loss statement. It helps users explore performance across 2024 and 2025, including revenue growth, profit margins, expense patterns, branch contributions, and departmental costs.

# **Business Problem**
FinSight Distribution & Consumer Products Ltd. recorded substantial revenue growth between 2024 and 2025. However, gross profit increased only modestly, operating expenses rose significantly, and the loss before tax widened. This indicates that higher revenue did not translate into stronger profitability.
Management requires a clearer understanding of the financial and operational factors behind this trend, including changes in profit margins, expenses, monthly performance, branch revenue, and departmental costs.

# **Project Objective**
This project analyses the company’s financial and operational performance across 2024 and 2025 using interactive Power BI dashboards and a structured Profit & Loss statement. It examines revenue growth, gross profit margins, operating expenses, and profit before tax over time and across branches and departments.
The analysis is intended to help management identify significant performance trends and areas for further review, supporting informed decisions on cost control, margin improvement, and operational efficiency.

# **Key Observations**

- Revenue increased by approximately **52.63%** from 2024 to 2025.
- Gross profit increased by approximately **6.40%**, much more slowly than revenue.
- Gross profit margin fell from approximately **16.73% in 2024** to **11.66% in 2025**.
- Operating expenses increased substantially between the two years.
- Net losses increased, indicating that revenue growth did not translate into improved overall profitability.
- Direct costs, occupancy costs, and personnel costs are among the largest expense groups shown in the dashboard.
- The branch and department visuals help identify where revenue is generated and where operating expenses are concentrated.

# **Strategic Recommendations**

1. **Review cost of sales and pricing**:Examine procurement costs, product pricing, and the mix of products sold to help improve gross profit margin.

2. **Strengthen expense controls**:Review major expense groups and departments regularly, set budgets, and investigate significant changes.

3. **Monitor branch and department performance**:Compare revenue and costs together so management can focus on sustainable profitability, not revenue alone.

# **Conclusion**

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Power BI can transform financial transaction data into clear, interactive reports that support financial and operational analysis. The dashboards bring together key indicators for revenue, profitability, expenses, branch performance, and departmental costs across 2024 and 2025.

The findings show that revenue growth alone did not lead to improved profitability. The increase in expenses and the widening loss before tax highlight the need for management to examine cost drivers and margins closely. By presenting these trends in an accessible format, the project supports further investigation and more informed decisions on expense control, margin improvement, and operational efficiency.
